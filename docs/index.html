Questions & Answers

A few things to know.

What is Hibi Lens?

Hibi Lens is an iPhone app that identifies everyday objects from photos and turns them into Japanese vocabulary cards. The name also appears as HibiLens in the website and GitHub URLs.

How does Hibi Lens turn a photo into a vocabulary card?

Take a photo or choose one from your library. Hibi Lens identifies a supported everyday object, then shows its Japanese term, reading, meaning, and pronunciation.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image recognition and vocabulary lookup happen on your device. Your photos, cards, and learning progress stay there.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Hibi Lens is designed to work entirely on your iPhone.

What information is included on a card?

A card can include the Japanese word, kana, kanji, romaji, meaning, and pronunciation, depending on the word.
